---
name: positioning
description: Work out what this product actually is, who it is for, and why anyone would switch to it. Use when the founder cannot explain their product in one sentence, before writing any marketing copy, or when preparing to pitch.
---

Use the `growth-marketer` subagent.

Read `docs/requirements.md` and skim the code to see what genuinely exists.

Produce, in the conversation (do not write a file):

1. **The one-liner** - "<product> helps <specific user> <do specific job> without <the annoying part of today's workaround>." Give three candidates, then recommend one and say why.
2. **Who it is not for** - two groups who will not care, and why. A product for everyone is a product for no one.
3. **The switch cost** - what the user has to give up or change to adopt this. Be honest about it.
4. **The proof** - the single most concrete thing that could be demonstrated in 30 seconds to make a stranger believe it works.
5. **The weakest claim** - the assertion in the one-liner most likely to be false, and the cheapest way to test it this week.

If the product does not yet do anything a stranger would care about, say that plainly instead of writing positioning for it.
